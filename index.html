HOW TO PLAY:
• Explore randomly generated levels, kill all enemies to unlock the EXIT PORTAL.
• Collect 🪙 coins, 💙 XP, ❤️ hearts, 🔫 weapons. Open chests, find HIDDEN CRACKED WALLS (shoot them!).
• Level up & choose 1 of 3 power-ups. Use shrines: ⛲ heal, 🎲 gamble, 🛒 merchant, 💀 cursed.
• Every 3rd floor (or Boss Rush) = BOSS FIGHT! Dodge radial bullets.
• PC: WASD + Mouse, E ability, SPACE dash, Q swap weapon, F interact.
• Mobile: Left joystick MOVE, Right joystick AIM+SHOOT, buttons for ability/dash.
• Spend coins in HUB to unlock heroes/weapons/worlds & upgrades. Good luck, Raider!
